# DanceMatch3 music sourcing notes

Updated: 2026-10-20

## Important licensing decision

Do not use Pixabay tracks in the ML training set. Although its audio is described as free to use, Pixabay's published guidance says its content may not be used to train machine-learning models or be incorporated into AI tools. See:
- https://pixabay.com/blog/posts/intellectual-property-explained-441/
- https://pixabay.com/service/terms/

The file supplemental_audio_sources.csv contains a small starter set of tracks with explicit CC BY 4.0 or CC0 licensing. The separate music_source_candidates.csv is a research list, not a ready-to-train dataset.

## Source quality

- Bachata: only a small number of clearly identified, openly licensed examples were found. The Sound Stabs track is electronic and must be listened to before using it as a Bachata label.
- Salsa: the Alex Morgan track is CC BY 4.0 but marked AI-generated by Free Music Archive. Ifness offers a seven-track Latin jazz/Salsa instrumental collection under CC BY 4.0; download tracks manually and verify each one by listening. The Zenodo Salsa Dataset contains annotated Salsa material, but its license/permission for this use is not clear from the landing page, so it is marked permission-required.
- Discofox: openly licensed disco/funk/dance tracks are available, but the sources do not label them as Discofox. Because Discofox can be danced to various 4/4 pop/disco tracks, they are weak candidates only. Do not treat them as gold labels until a knowledgeable dancer listens and confirms them.

## Download and label protocol

1. Download only from the listed source pages and keep a copy of the license/source evidence.
2. Do not redistribute the raw tracks in the public GitHub repository.
3. Before training, listen to each candidate and set its label status to verified or exclude it.
4. Split train/test by track, not by 15-second clip, to prevent near-identical sections of the same song leaking across both splits.
5. Build a larger independent set for each dance style; two or three tracks per style are not enough to support a reliable accuracy claim.
6. Credit CC BY tracks in the project documentation. CC0 tracks do not require attribution, though source records should still be kept.
